# Workspace working agreements

## Delegation

- Use Space Bunny Alpha (`openrouter/stealth/space-bunny-alpha` through OpenCode) for worker subagents on workspace tasks and for research behind workspace questions.
- The primary assistant acts as orchestrator: give workers scoped tasks, review and refine their results, reconcile changes, and verify the final answer or implementation.
- If Space Bunny Alpha is unavailable, say so instead of silently using a different worker model.

## Commits

- Commit completed, verified work in small logical milestones as it progresses; do not leave all commits until the whole phase is finished.
- Write concise, human-sounding commit messages. Do not use conventional-commit prefixes such as `fix:`, `chore:`, or `feat:`.
